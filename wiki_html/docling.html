Docling decision flow

Docling turns PDFs, office documents, images, and audio into structured documents. The useful design question is not “which pipeline is strongest?” but “which evidence must this document preserve?”

02

Choose the Smallest Sufficient Pipeline

Native or mixed PDF?

Use native text where available and OCR bitmap regions or scanned pages. Mixed documents do not require two manual conversions.

Tables or diagrams?

Enable table structure when cell relationships matter. Generate pictures or use a PDF VLM pipeline when diagrams carry evidence.

Private and CPU-only?

Use local OCR, disable remote services, avoid unnecessary page-image generation, and budget extra time for high-resolution scans.

03

Chunking and Provenance

HybridChunker respects document structure while fitting a token budget. A chunk can contain multiple document items; each item can retain page number, bounding box, and character span.

for item in chunk.meta.doc_items:
    for prov in getattr(item, "prov", []):
        print(prov.page_no, prov.bbox, prov.charspan)

DOCX does not expose stable page geometry. Convert to PDF when citations must point to exact pages or bounding boxes.
